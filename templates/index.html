💬 Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I activate my Premium status after buying?

After a successful secure PayPal payment, click the "Contact Me on Discord" button to open a support ticket. Send your payment confirmation, and you will receive a unique license key. Then simply type /redeem [your_key] on your server!

❓ Can I transfer my Lifetime license to another server?

Yes! Premium status can be easily bound to your specific server or your profile. If you decide to change or rebuild your server, our team will help you transfer your active license free of charge.

❓ What happens if VoltBot goes offline?

VoltBot is hosted on high-performance cloud servers with a 99.9% uptime guarantee. In the extremely rare event of maintenance downtime, all premium durations are automatically extended so you never lose a single minute of your paid time.
